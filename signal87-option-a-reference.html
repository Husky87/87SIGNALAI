  • containerFlex child of the shell, never position: fixed. 1px --rule-2 top border, --card at 94% with backdrop blur.
  • padding9px top, 6px sides, bottom is max(10px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom)) — 34px on a notched iPhone, 10px on an SE.
  • itemEqual flex, min 44px tall, centred column: 22px icon over a 9px mono uppercase label, 5px gap.
  • statesInactive --slate outline. Active --ink filled. No pill, no underline.
  • New30px circle in --mark, offset 4px above its neighbours. Opens the picker and starts a thread.
  • behaviourTab switches preserve scroll position and do not refetch. Dock hides only in the document viewer.
